Mother's Day Special

The Lyle Hadlock Mother's Day special is an annual event where Lyle offers a certain CD for church units at the special rate of $2 each - plus shipping.

This year Lyle is offering his brand new CD Simply Sacred at this special price.

Please Read Before Ordering:
  • All orders are by credit card only and orders will continue to be accepted while supplies last.
  • Each CD is contained in a factory printed cardstock case.
  • DELIVERY orders will be shipped (UPS Ground) starting April 27th. (Mother's Day is May 13th).
       - Cost of shipping is around $18 per 100 CDs inside Utah or $23 per 100 outside Utah.
  • PICKUP orders will be available starting April 30th in Ogden, UT.
       - An email will be sent before that time with instructions.
       - Cost of handling is $10 for the entire order.
